Screens now change by plan and by role

29 October 2026. Built with a multi-agent workflow: a shared core, four page areas in parallel, an independent check of each area with every gap fixed, and a final type-check, build and test run. Then verified by signing in to the running app. The public site was not touched, and pricing is unchanged.

What is different now
  • The menu follows the plan. Screens the organisation's plan doesn't include are no longer in the menu: no tags, no previews. An upgrade makes them appear at once. The plan chip (for example "Guarded · L1") shows in the top bar on every page.
  • The menu follows the role. An approver sees 14–15 screens, a viewer 25–29 and an auditor 22–26, against 37–41 for an owner. Typing the address of a hidden screen shows "Your role cannot open this".
  • Pages follow both.
    • Buttons a role can't use are now hidden, not greyed out.
    • Sections of higher plans are removed.
    • Links to screens the person can't open become plain text.
    • Upgrade prompts appear only to the owner, as one short "Change plan" line.
  • Today follows the role. An approver's Today starts with "Waiting for you": the approvals for their groups. The auditor lands on the Auditor workspace.
