Elan1 Govern · front end · level and role check

Does the platform change by level and by role?

29 October 2026. This was tested in the running app: I signed in as an owner, admin, approver and viewer at each of L1, L2, L3 and Sovereign, and as the auditor in each of the four client organisations (20 sign-ins in all). For each, I recorded the menu, which pages open, the state of key buttons and the level features. Invoices were read from the same billing code the Meter page uses. Nothing was changed. The public site is excluded.

Answer: yes, it works both ways.
  • By level. Each organisation's assurance level changes which features are live, which screens show only a preview and upgrade path, how the platform is hosted and keys are held, and which items appear on its invoice.
  • By role. Each person's role changes the menu, the pages they can open and the buttons they can use. This works the same way at all four levels.
  • One problem in the demo data, now fixed. At L2, L3 and Sovereign, the Finance and Procurement approvers were also recorded as owners of their group's agents, so the same-person rule stopped them approving most of their own group's calls. Those agents are now owned by the organisation's admin, and the approvers can decide their group's calls again (§4).

  • Blocked pages. These show "Your role cannot open this", with the reason.

No approver now sees "You own the agent that proposed this". The type-check and build pass, and the 78 scripted checks pass with 0 failures. The original finding is kept below for the record.

At L2, L3 and Sovereign, Finance approvers cannot approve most of their own group's payments.

  • What happens. In the demo data, the Finance approvers own their organisation's Finance agents:
    • Gaurav owns ap-agent and receivables-agent (Northbank);
    • Revathi owns supplier-payments and patient-billing (Kaveri);
    • Prakash owns ap-processor and loan-collections (Deccan).
    From L2 the same-person rule forbids approving a call from an agent you own. So these approvers see "You own the agent that proposed this, so you cannot also approve it" on most Finance payments.
  • Tested. Revathi could approve 1 of 12 waiting items, and Prakash 1 of 11. Gaurav could approve 1; at Northbank most of the rest are also held by the stop. At L1 the same overlap only raises a flag, so Venkat can still approve.
  • Why it matters. The rule itself is right and was confirmed by you. But it makes the README's "decide held payments" journey hard to walk for those three demo approvers.
  • Suggested fix (demo data only). Make an owner or admin the owner of those Finance agents, for example the organisation's admin, rather than the Finance approver. The rule stays unchanged, and the approvers can then decide their group's payments as the journey describes.
