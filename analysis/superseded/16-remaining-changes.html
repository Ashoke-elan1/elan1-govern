B. The 13 side effects of the earlier fixes

5L3, SovMediumOnboarding locks key and level changes for 30 days. When a new L3 or Sovereign organisation is onboarded, the key ceremony now publishes a successor key that only takes over after the 30-day overlap. Until then, further key changes and level changes are refused. Change: the first ceremony during onboarding creates the organisation's first own key and signs with it at once. There is no earlier key to overlap with. Later rotations keep the 30-day overlap. onboarding/complete.ts:104; api/index.ts:686; mock/platform.ts:768; mock/keys.ts:353
