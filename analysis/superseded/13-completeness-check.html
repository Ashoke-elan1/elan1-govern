Completeness check — after the fixes

28 October 2026, late night. Nothing was built or changed for this check. It is the second check of the night, run after the 45 fixes in 12-fix-report. It covers the console, the receipt verifier, the developer portal, the counterparty, regulator and partner portals, and the status, legal, fingerprint, maintenance and offline pages, plus every demo sign-in, at L1, L2, L3 and Sovereign. The public site is not included: it is already built.

How it was checked.
  • Code reviews. Five reviewers each read one area against the platform plan and the four document digests. The areas were: Work and access; Records; Agents and Sovereign; Assurance and billing; and the developer portal with journeys and links. Each reviewer did two things: (a) confirmed the previous findings in their area were really fixed, and (b) looked for anything still missing or newly wrong. I re-checked the most important findings in the code myself.
  • Sign-in check. Every demo account was tried in the running app with its published password.
  • Links. A static check matched every link target against the routes. The live crawl run after the fixes still applies, because the app code has not changed since.
Not counted:
  • the rulings in 08-conflict-decisions.md, and the choices in §4 of 10-build-report and §4 of 12-fix-report, all confirmed by you;
  • items the memorandum marks LATER;
  • the public site;
  • backend internals.

All screens exist, all links resolve and all 30 demo sign-ins work. Of the 45 earlier findings, the reviewers confirmed 34 as fully fixed. The other 11 are fixed in their main place but left a follow-on gap: findings 5, 13, 14, 16, 18, 24, 30, 32, 33, 38 and 41.

Most of the 42 findings this round are second-order: places the night's fixes didn't reach, or side effects of a fix. The ones that matter most:

  • Pack checks refuse held calls. An admitted pack's check refuses a call that the rulebook has already held for a named person, instead of leaving it for that person to decide. It also ignores the pack's configured values.
  • "Everything" exports at L2+. They can't clear the new integrity-run requirement while live traffic flows: every new call puts the newest record beyond the last check.
  • Proof › Keys at L2. An approved KMS rotation shows as still waiting, with an approve button that fails. This is a side effect of fix 24.
  • L2 seals. Every L2 record is sealed with autonomy level A3, although no level exists below L3.
  • Sovereign egress. SAML, connectors (public MCP servers, Gmail, Zoho cloud) and Elan1's hosted maintenance banner still reach outside the network.
  • Counterparty seats. Past invoices are rewritten when seats change.
  • The legal sub-processor page. It now says something false about WhatsApp.

  • Pack admissions. The demo has none seeded, so the pack-check findings (#8, #9) appear only after an admission is run by hand.
  • Partial reads. Some large screens were read in part: the counterparty drawers, disclosure, upgrade stages and Path to production.
